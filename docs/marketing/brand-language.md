# Stagenum brand language

**Status:** Adopted
**Adopted:** September 16, 2026

This document is the source of truth for the product name, pronunciation,
slogan, and approved campaign language.

## Product name

**Stagenum**

- Pronunciation: **STAY-juh-num**
- Construction: **stage** + **num**, with a secondary association to **enum**
  and an ordered sequence of project stages.
- Preserve the complete word **stage** in spelling and presentation.
- Use `Stagenum` in prose and `stagenum` where lowercase identifiers are
  required.

## Durable slogan

> **Stagenum — Make every stage count.**

This is the primary brand lockup and should be favored in repository,
prototype, website, app-store, and general product materials.

## General campaign copy

> **It’s quick. Review, pay, & done — Stagenum.**

This is the approved conversational second-level line. It expresses the client
interaction after a provider submits completed work for review.

Contextual variants may shorten the final beat when the surrounding interface
already establishes that review and approval occurred:

- **Pay, & done.**
- **Paid—and done.**

## Supporting descriptor

> Proof-backed stage payments for independent work.

The descriptor may accompany the product name where the coined name needs a
plain-language explanation. It is explanatory copy, not a replacement for the
durable slogan.

## Previous working name

`StagePaid` was the product's initial working name. It should appear only in
historical naming records, Git history, or text that explains the rebrand. New
product-facing material must use `Stagenum`.
