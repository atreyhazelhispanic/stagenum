**Status:** Proposed

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

**Decision owners:** Stagenum maintainers

## Context

What forces, constraints, and product requirements make this decision
necessary? Describe the problem without assuming the selected solution.

## Decision

State the decision in direct, testable language.

## Decision details

Define the boundaries, rules, and implementation expectations needed to apply
the decision consistently.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier, safer, or more explicit?

### Negative and tradeoffs

- What cost, limitation, or operational burden do we accept?

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative name

Explain why the alternative was not selected for the current context. Avoid
claiming that it is universally inferior.

## Security and privacy implications

Describe how the decision affects authorization, data exposure, secrets,
auditing, isolation, and failure behavior.

## Validation and enforcement

Explain how tests, tooling, review, monitoring, or operational checks will show
that the decision is being followed.

## Revisit triggers

List observable conditions that would justify a new ADR. Do not use an
arbitrary date as the only reason to revisit a decision.
