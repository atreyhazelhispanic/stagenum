# Contractor Workflow Questionnaire

**Estimated time:** 20–25 minutes

## Purpose

Thank you for helping with this research. We are studying how residential
contractors document project stages, confirm completion with homeowners, and
move completed work into billing.

This is product research, not a sales call or an evaluation of you or your
business. There are no right answers. Specific examples from work you have
already completed are more useful than describing an ideal process.

## Privacy

Please do not include:

- homeowner or employee names;
- exact property or job-site addresses;
- account, card, or bank information;
- contractually confidential information; or
- copies of contracts, invoices, photographs, or private messages.

Approximate amounts and time ranges are welcome when they help explain the
workflow. Your response will be assigned an anonymous participant code in the
research notes.

By returning this questionnaire, you acknowledge that your answers may be used
in anonymized product-research notes. Your identity or an attributable quotation
will not be published without separate permission.

## Your role and business

1. What is your role, what kinds of residential projects make up most of the
   business's work, and approximately how is the team structured?
2. Which parts do you personally handle: progress documentation, homeowner
   communication, approval, invoicing, or payment follow-up?

## One recent project

Please answer the remaining questions using one residential project that was
completed or actively billed within the last 12 months and had more than one
phase, milestone, or progress payment.

3. What type of project was it, approximately how long did it last, and how was
   it divided into stages or payment milestones?
4. Where were those stages documented, and how did you and the homeowner know
   what counted as complete?
5. Did the agreed stages or expectations change after work began? What happened?

## Evidence and homeowner communication

6. What photos, notes, checklists, or other evidence did you capture, where was
   it stored, and how did the homeowner receive it?
7. Describe a time completed work became difficult to inspect or you had to find
   old evidence and reconstruct what happened.

## Review and approval

8. How did the homeowner learn that a stage was ready, and what specifically
   counted as approval?
9. Describe a recent time approval was unclear or delayed. About how long did it
   take, what follow-up was needed, and what effect did it have?

## Changes and additional scope

10. Describe the most recent time a homeowner requested a change. How was it
    recorded, and how did you decide whether it corrected agreed work or added
    new work?
11. If the change affected price or schedule, how was the updated agreement
    documented?

## Invoice and payment handoff

12. What triggered the milestone or progress invoice, who created it, and which
    tool and source information did they use?
13. What information had to be copied between messages, photographs, notes, or
    software?
14. Approximately how much time passed between completion, approval, and invoice
    issuance? What delayed invoicing or payment, and did the homeowner understand
    which work the invoice represented?

## Current tools and cost of the process

15. Which tools were used from agreement through payment, what did each do, and
    which record was treated as the source of truth?
16. What works well enough that you would not replace it, and what does the
    overall process cost in time, delayed payment, rework, disputes, or client
    trust? Approximate ranges are sufficient.

## Product concept

Please answer the questions above before reading this section.

Stagenum is exploring a focused workflow where a contractor defines a payable
project stage, attaches evidence when it is complete, and sends it to the
homeowner for approval or a documented Change Request. Approval prepares a draft
invoice for the contractor to review and issue.

17. Which part fits the project you described, what would not fit, and what might
    the homeowner find confusing or burdensome?
18. Which existing tool would Stagenum need to work with, and what measurable
    result would make changing your process worthwhile?

## Follow-up

19. Would you be willing to spend 15–20 minutes on a follow-up call to clarify
    your answers or review a clickable prototype?
20. If a prototype addressed your concerns, would you consider testing it on an
    appropriate future project? Is there another contractor or coordinator with
    a different process whom we should ask?

Thank you for taking the time to provide a detailed response. Please return the
completed questionnaire through the same private channel in which you received
it rather than posting it publicly.
