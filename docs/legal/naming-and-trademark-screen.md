# Naming and trademark screen

## Adopted replacement name

**Decision date:** September 16, 2026
**Adopted product name:** Stagenum
**Pronunciation:** STAY-juh-num
**Durable slogan:** Make every stage count.

Following the StagePaid risk assessment recorded below, the project adopted
`Stagenum` as its replacement working brand. The founder-led preliminary screen
found no exact commercial product in the intended market, no exact Apple App
Store or Google Play listing, and no exact federal wordmark record in the
official USPTO search system on the decision date. Public RDAP queries returned
no registration record for `stagenum.com`, `stagenum.app`, `stagenum.io`,
`stagenum.co`, or `stagenum.net` at the time checked.

General web results for `stagenum` and `stageNum` were predominantly technical
uses meaning "stage number," rather than source-identifying commercial uses.
Close-spelling searches did not surface a directly competing payment,
invoicing, contractor, or freelance-work product.

These findings support adopting Stagenum for continued product research and
prototype development. They do not constitute legal clearance, establish
domain availability at purchase time, or replace a comprehensive trademark
search by qualified counsel before commercial launch or material brand
investment.

## Previous working-name screen

**Screen date:** September 16, 2026
**Proposed name:** StagePaid / StagePaid LLC
**Intended product:** Software for contractors and clients to document staged work, review evidence, approve work, issue invoices, and process payments.

## Purpose and limits

This document records a preliminary founder-led name screen. It is not a legal opinion, a comprehensive trademark clearance search, or a substitute for advice from a qualified trademark attorney.

Business-name availability and trademark clearance answer different questions. A state may permit an LLC name even when using that name as a product brand could create trademark or marketplace-confusion risk.

## Material findings

### 1. Stagepay.app

An active product at [stagepay.app](https://stagepay.app/) describes invoicing and payment management software. Its published feature set includes staged payments, invoice creation, automated reminders, Stripe credit-card payments, and workflow automation. These functions substantially overlap StagePaid's proposed payment-stage and invoicing workflow.

Source: [Stagepay key features](https://help.stagepay.app/en/articles/30-key-features-of-stagepay)

### 2. StagePay mobile app for performers

An active Apple App Store product uses the name `StagePay`. It provides invoicing, expense tracking, payment tracking, reminders, and a no-sign-in client portal. Its description expressly includes freelancers and US billing formats. This is a direct software and app-store-channel overlap even though performers are its initial niche.

Sources: [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/stagepay/id6766096255), [product website](https://stagesofpay.com/)

### 3. StagePay for freelancers and contractors

An active product at [getstagepay.co](https://getstagepay.co/) markets invoicing and payment follow-up to freelancers, contractors, architects, engineers, and small businesses. Its features include invoices, deposits, reminders, PDF exports, and payment tracking. This closely overlaps both the intended users and commercial purpose of StagePaid.

### 4. Existing companies and business names outside New Mexico

The screen also found:

- [STAGEPAY LTD](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/13689987), an active UK software-development company incorporated in 2021.
- `STAGEPAY`, an active Australian business name recorded from 2024 under Hart Property Development Pty Ltd in the [Australian Business Register](https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View?abn=54610061142).

These foreign entities do not by themselves decide US trademark rights, but they show that the name is already used commercially in payment-related contexts.

## Preliminary risk assessment

**Recommendation: do not treat `StagePaid` as cleared.**

`StagePaid` and `StagePay` are likely to sound identical in ordinary speech. The difference between `Paid` and `Pay` may not reliably distinguish the marks when the products occupy the same invoicing, staged-payment, contractor, freelancer, and client-payment market. The active app-store and web products create practical risks even without an exact federal registration, including:

- customer and recruiter confusion;
- app-store discoverability and approval friction;
- domain and social-handle constraints;
- objections or claims based on earlier use;
- an expensive rebrand after users, code, documents, and goodwill accumulate;
- a weaker ability to claim a distinctive brand of StagePaid's own.

For this preliminary screen, the risk is rated **high enough to pause formation and public launch under the StagePaid brand** pending either a professional clearance review or selection of a more distinctive name.

## What the clean New Mexico search means

The official [New Mexico business portal](https://enterprise.sos.nm.gov/search/business) returned no results for the two searched spellings. That is useful evidence that the entity name may be available in New Mexico, but it is not permission to use the name as a national software brand and does not establish trademark priority.

## Recommended next action

1. Do not file `StagePaid LLC`, purchase major branding, or publish to app stores solely on the basis of the New Mexico result.
2. Preserve `StagePaid` as the project's working title while evaluating distinctive replacement names, if desired.
3. Screen each serious candidate across USPTO records, state entities, common-law web use, domains, social handles, and app stores before adopting it.
4. For any finalist worth investing in, ask a US trademark attorney for a comprehensive clearance search and written risk assessment covering the proposed software and payment services.
5. Record the final naming decision and supporting evidence in this document or a superseding decision record.

## Search limitations

- There is no single authoritative US registry that provides a complete national search of every state business entity, trade name, and unregistered brand.
- Exact-name searches do not capture every confusingly similar spelling, pronunciation, logo, translation, or related service.
- Search results and product availability can change after the screen date.
- Trademark rights may arise through use without a federal registration.
- This screen did not determine ownership, priority dates, geographic scope, or enforceability for every discovered use.
